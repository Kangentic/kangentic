# Command Terminal

The Command Terminal provides quick access to Claude Code without creating a task on the board. Useful for one-off actions like creating releases, running queries, or any ad-hoc interaction.

**Opening:** Press `Ctrl+Shift+P` (or `Cmd+Shift+P` on macOS), or click the terminal icon in the title bar (the left-most icon in its right-hand button row). The same button **toggles** the panel closed again, so there is always a one-click way to hide it, even when the panel is maximized. The terminal icon reflects activity across your open terminals: its prompt blinks in green while an agent is working, it holds a steady warm amber when one needs your input, and it stays plain when idle. `Ctrl+Tab` and `Ctrl+Shift+Tab` also move to a Command Terminal terminal, opening the panel if it is hidden. See [Keyboard shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.md).

**The panel:** the Command Terminal is one panel over a slight backdrop blur. A rail runs down its left side, with **Add terminal** at its top and your earlier conversations below, and your terminals fill the rest.
- Drag the panel by its header, resize it from any edge or corner, and maximize or restore it with its header button, a double-click on the header, or `Ctrl+Shift+M`. Its place **persists globally** across all projects and app restarts.
- **Size the rail.** Drag the rail's right edge to widen it (up to 400px) or narrow it (down to 200px). Past 200 it stays at its narrowest for a while, so you can let go there without folding it; keep going and it folds to a thin strip before you let go, and dragging back out opens it again. **Hide**, the rail's bottom row, folds it, and the strip's button in the same spot opens it again. Folded, the strip keeps **Add terminal** as an icon and, shows each of your terminals as the same activity icon a task card shows, so you can see which one is working or waiting on you; click one to bring it to the front, or right-click it for its menu. The rail is part of the panel: widening, narrowing, folding or opening it makes the whole panel wider or narrower by the same amount, and your terminals keep their size. In a maximized panel, or the panel in a window of its own, the terminals make the room instead. The rail's width and fold are kept across all projects and app restarts. In a narrow panel the rail shows narrower so the terminals keep their room, and goes back to your width when there is space.
- Spawns Claude Code at the project root on the configured default base branch. If you have uncommitted changes to tracked files on another branch, a new terminal stays on that branch and tells you so, rather than carrying your work onto the base.
- **Run up to four at once.** Click **Add terminal** for another (up to four per project, counting any a paired phone started); it turns off once you hit the cap, and its tip says why. The panel lays them out for you: one fills the panel, two sit side by side, three are one tall beside two stacked, and four make a two by two grid. Each terminal keeps at least 630px, about 77 columns, so resizing the panel stops where they all still fit, and adding a terminal grows the panel to make room for it. The bar under a terminal is always one row: the agent, model, effort, rate limits and context stay, and anything that does not fit moves into the **+N** pill at its end; click it to see the rest. In a window too small for that, they all still show, narrower; maximize the panel for more room. Drag a seam between two terminals to resize them, or double-click it to even them; the sizes you set hold until a terminal starts or stops.
- **Expand one.** With two or more running, a terminal's **Expand** button (right end of its header) fills the panel with it. The others keep running behind it. **Running** in the rail lists every terminal, the one in front highlighted: click one's name to bring it to the front (expanded, or focused while they tile), or its stop button to stop it. Right-click one for its whole `...` menu without bringing it up. While one is expanded you can resize the panel down to a single terminal; **Restore the grid** brings the tiles back and widens the panel again if they need it.
- **Swap two.** Drag a terminal by its header onto another terminal's tile: a picture of it the size of your smallest terminal follows the pointer, the tile it will land in shows empty with a blue border, and the terminal there moves into its old place so you see the swap before you make it. Let go to swap them, or press `Escape` to put everything back.
- **Each terminal is numbered.** A terminal titles itself `Command Terminal 1`, `Command Terminal 2`, and so on, from its durable slot, so two terminals are tellable apart and the number stays put when a sibling opens or closes. The same title identifies that terminal on its Agent Monitor row. With Claude Code or Kimi, once you send a first prompt, the title becomes a short auto-derived name for what you asked instead; other agents keep the numbered title.
- **Rename a terminal.** Click the pencil after its title, or choose **Rename** in its `...` menu (or in a Running row's right-click menu), type a name, and press `Enter`. `Escape` cancels, and saving an empty name puts back `Command Terminal N`. A name you give always wins over the automatic one, shows on the terminal's Agent Monitor row and on your phone, and stays with the conversation in Earlier.
- The title always wins the header's space: the quick-action pills (Commands, Project, Changes, shortcuts) fold into the `...` menu as a terminal narrows.
- The **branch picker** in the header lets you switch branches. Selecting a new branch kills that terminal's session and respawns it on the selected branch. The pill names the branch the checkout is actually on: it is re-read from git whenever you reopen the panel and whenever the checkout moves (an agent running `git checkout` inside the terminal, or your own git usage), so it never keeps claiming a branch the repo has left. Reopening never checks anything out.
- The terminal's **Changes** panel measures ahead/behind, and diffs its Branch tab, against the project's default base branch, the same base the pill's default names.

**Earlier conversations:** when you stop a terminal you typed into, its conversation moves to **Earlier** in the rail, newest first, with the terminal's name and when it ended. A terminal that was never named (naming needs an Automation agent, set in Settings > Workflows) shows the first thing you asked it instead. Until the first one ends, the rail shows no Earlier heading at all. Click one to pick it up again with its name kept. If the terminal you are focused on is fresh and untouched, the conversation replaces it in place; otherwise it opens in a new terminal beside your others, so a terminal you have used is never replaced. The rail shows as many as fit, and hovering a name the rail cuts short shows all of it. A conversation leaves the list while a terminal has it open, and for good once its agent's own history is gone. A terminal you never typed into leaves nothing to resume.

**Open in new window:** the panel's **Open in new window** button moves the whole panel into its own window for that project, titled "Command Terminal - Project". The terminals keep running and carry straight over. While it is open, `Ctrl+Shift+P` and the title-bar icon bring that window forward instead of opening the panel in the app. Closing the window brings the panel back to the app with your terminals, unless you closed it by stopping the last one. Stopping the last terminal in the window closes the window.

**Ending and pausing:**
- **A terminal ends when its agent does.** Type `/exit`, or let the agent crash, and the terminal ends and closes within a few seconds, so you are not left at a bare shell prompt (under WSL, `/exit` ends the terminal about 5 seconds later). `/clear` keeps the terminal. Anything its agent started in the background (a dev server, a watcher) is stopped about 5 seconds after the terminal ends and listed in the leftover-processes toast, the same as when a task finishes; with **Stop Leftover Processes** off it is only listed.
- **No pause ends a terminal.** The **When idle** and **When memory runs low** pauses both skip Command Terminals, so a terminal waiting on you stays up.
- **A paired phone can start and stop terminals, and resume earlier conversations.** A terminal the phone starts in the project you have open gets its place the next time you open the panel; the title-bar icon counts it right away.
- A shimmer overlay shows while Claude Code initializes, then lifts to reveal the clean TUI.
- Command Terminals are independent of task sessions: they don't appear in the terminal panel tabs and produce no toasts when they exit or go idle. The one exception is a terminal whose shell never starts: it shows one error toast and closes. Each running Command Terminal still holds an agent slot under Max Concurrent Sessions, as a task's agent does.
- Your terminals are **preserved across project switches**. If you open terminals, switch to another project, and switch back, they are still running. Each project keeps its own terminals, so you can keep ad-hoc work going while navigating between projects.
- If git checkout fails when switching branches (e.g., uncommitted changes), a warning toast explains the issue and the session stays on the current branch.

**Hiding vs stopping:** Press `Ctrl+Shift+P` again, `Ctrl+Shift+W`, click the panel's hide button, or click the blurred backdrop to **hide** the panel. Every terminal keeps running in the background, and reopening reattaches each one, right where you left it. There is no per-terminal close button: a terminal's **Stop** (red, and the `...` menu's "Stop terminal") **ends** that one terminal. It kills the PTY, cleans up the session directory, and closes it; the rest stay open. Its conversation moves to Earlier if you typed into it. Stopping the last terminal hides the panel.
